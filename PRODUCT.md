# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Game Developers and Pixel Artists who need a fast, specialized in-browser drawing tool.

## Product Purpose
A fast, specialized pixel art editor in the browser, meant for creating, editing, optimizing and exporting pixel art directly without heavy desktop software.

## Brand Personality
Professional, dark mode, minimalist, and performance-focused (reminiscent of desktop tools like Photoshop / Aseprite).

## Anti-references
- "Toy-like" or flashy interfaces.
- Modern startup "flat" design or SaaS templates (excessive whitespace, large fonts, heavy drop shadows).
- Over-rounded cards or floating ghost-card UI.

## Design Principles
- **Tool, not a toy**: Prioritize workspace density and utility over marketing whitespace. The canvas is the hero.
- **Performance First**: The UI should feel instant and never get in the way of the creative workflow.
- **Expert Confidence**: Use established industry iconography and robust keyboard shortcut support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 (AA) contrast compliance; ensure text and icons are clearly legible against the dark background.
- Full keyboard shortcut support for rapid professional workflow.
